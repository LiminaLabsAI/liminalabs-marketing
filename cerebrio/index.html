Cerebrio · The understanding layer

The reasoning layer.

What the organisation knows, and what changed.

Memory here is constructed, not retrieved. Traces are parsed, contextualised, reasoned over and cross-examined before being promoted to understanding the next piece of work can rely on.

How it runs

Inside your own agent stack.

With or without the other two. Cerebrio grounds whatever agents you already run — it does not require Intent Studio or Precepta to be useful.

What it is for

Judgement that outlives the project.

When a senior person leaves, their reasoning leaves with them. Cerebrio is where an organisation's understanding is kept, versioned and cross-examined — so the next team starts from what the last one learned rather than from nothing.
