Company

Named for the thing it sells.

Limina is the Latin plural of limen: thresholds. A threshold is not a wall — things cross it, in both directions — and it still marks exactly where one place stops and another begins. That is what we build: a boundary a probabilistic system cannot argue its way past, drawn around it rather than asked of it.

What we believe

Sovereignty is not about hosting weights. It is about owning your reasoning. Models are the muscle — rented, replaceable, changing under you. What must not be interchangeable is the mind: what your organisation is trying to do, what it is allowed to do, and what it has learned. Build the boundary outside the model, where the model cannot argue past it, and the rest follows.
