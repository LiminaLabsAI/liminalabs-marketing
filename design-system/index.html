Limina Labs · Design system · v1.0

Every surface shows what is underneath.

Limen turns the Limina mark into an interface. Four bars become the rhythm, one 42.5° lean becomes the only diagonal in the system, and the cobalt ramp becomes every accent. Glass is not styling here — it is the product's promise rendered literally: you can see through it, and it still holds.

Foundations

Nothing here was chosen for taste alone.

Every value comes from something already in the brand kit: the ramps are the mark's four fills, the grounds are sampled from the deck backgrounds, the ink is lifted out of the lockup, and the spacing scale is the mark's own geometry.

Geometry and rhythm

The mark's bars lean 22 units across 24 of height. That is 42.5°, and it is the only diagonal the system permits — accents, sweeps, stripes and step markers all use it, and nothing else tilts. The spacing scale is the same geometry: 12 is the gap between bars, 24 is a bar's height, 36 is the pitch from one bar to the next.

Surfaces

Three densities, and the choice between them is legibility, not taste. Use thin where the pane is a container, pane where content sits over the page, and solid where the content must stay readable over anything at all — menus, sheets, and the nav once it has scrolled.

Thin · 38%
Pane · 62%
Solid · 86%
Glass needs something to refract. The page's aura layer is structural, not decoration — over a flat fill, backdrop-filter produces a grey rectangle and every pane in the system collapses. It ships in base.css as body::before.

Components

Built to be assembled, not decorated.

Every component below is live. Switch the theme in the nav and check it again — light and dark are equal citizens here, not a mode and an afterthought.

Floating nav

Fixed and inset from every edge, so the page runs visibly underneath it. The current page takes a raised pill rather than an underline, and the bar thickens to solid once you scroll — the one at the top of this page is it.

Lift is the brand mark's only animation here: it plays once when the page loads (data-lim-lift) and once more each time you hover it (.lb-lift-hover). March stays in the system for other brand surfaces, but the nav does not use it.

Motion

One logo animation. Three motifs that may loop.

Lift is the only animation the logo itself is allowed, and it plays once. The other three are the bars alone — they carry no logo status, which is exactly why they are permitted to repeat.

Lift

0.66s, once. Launch, splash, hero, first slide.

March

2.4s, while hovered. Hover this card.

Trail

3.2s, loops. Loading and in-flight requests.

Relay

2.8s, loops. Multi-step progress.

Reduced motion is honoured system-wide. Under prefers-reduced-motion every animation stops and the sweep is removed — but nothing that carries meaning disappears. A ladder mid-progress still shows how far it got.

Setup

Two links, one script.

The system is plain CSS with custom properties — no build step, no framework, and nothing to compile. Drop design-system/ into a project and import the one entry file. The typefaces are self-hosted in fonts/, so the page makes no external requests at all. 

<!-- Set the theme before paint so there is no flash -->
<script>try{var t=localStorage.getItem("limina-theme");
  if(t)document.documentElement.dataset.theme=t;}catch(e){}</script>

<link rel="stylesheet" href="design-system/css/limina.css?v=6.2">

<!-- before </body> -->
<script src="design-system/js/limina.js?v=6.2" defer></script>

Theme

Follows the system unless someone chooses. data-theme="dark" on <html> wins in both directions, and the choice persists.

Overriding

Redefine tokens on :root after the import. Never override a component rule — if a component does not fit, it needs a modifier, not a patch.

Products

A product may take one colour from the ramp as its accent. It never gets its own mark, its own geometry, or a second diagonal.
