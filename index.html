The premise

Two things are true, and most of the industry is building as if neither were.

First premise

Context evaporates, and you pay for it again.

Enterprise AI today treats a foundation model as an end-to-end reasoning engine. When the context window closes, the operational context and the reasoning history close with it. Nothing survives the session but the output.

So the organisation pays a second time — the same GPU hours, the same tokens, the same logic path — to rediscover a conclusion it already reached last quarter. Repeated reasoning engineering is the largest silent line item in enterprise AI, and it grows with every deployment.

Second premise

Alignment cannot be finished inside the weights.

Perfect alignment through internal neural weights is not merely difficult. For systems expressive enough to be useful, it is uncomputable — behaviour stays irreducible, and at the margin it stays unpredictable. No amount of fine-tuning converts a black box into an auditable one.

Reliability, auditability and safety therefore have to be enforced from outside, by a deterministic structural harness that does not depend on the model behaving in order to hold.

Therefore We build the harness — and everything the harness leaves behind.

The instrument

Every vendor picks a point on the autonomy spectrum and hard-codes it.

Copilots stay propose-only — safe, and permanently limited, with a human as the throughput ceiling forever. Autonomous agents act by default — impressive in a demo, unsellable into a regulated process, and they ask you to accept the whole risk in a single leap. Autonomy is not a property of a product. It is a decision an enterprise makes per domain and per risk class, and changes its mind about.

Intent Studio↗

Turn intent into something that can be acted on.

Shipped

Translates a high-level enterprise goal into a formal, bounded intent model and a structural logic graph. Explicit constraints replace unstructured prompting, so ambiguity is resolved before execution rather than discovered twenty steps into it.

Precepta↗

Govern how your enterprise consumes AI.

Private beta

Every AI request the enterprise makes — from an agent, an app or a person — passes through one place that governs it, routes it and records it. Today most of that traffic is a black box: teams are already using models nobody approved, and the executive who owns the risk has no visibility into any of it.

Precepta makes it a glass box. Policy is enforced in line rather than reviewed afterwards: the firewall redacts, the policy engine decides, the router picks a model inside your boundary, output is scanned, and every request can be replayed — the decision and the reason for it.

It runs in your environment and nothing leaves it. Models are pluggable, so you are never locked to one vendor and never buying governance from the company whose model you are governing. Execution is also where the record gets made: each run emits the telemetry and decision traces the rest of the stack compounds.

The loop

Four products. One continuous learning system.

Intent Studio bounds what is actually being asked for, and refuses to let an agent fill the gaps with assumptions.

Precepta carries that intent into the live pipeline inside three gates, and emits the telemetry, decision traces and interaction trails the run produces.

Cerebrio compiles those traces into typed, dated, contestable structure — playbooks and a knowledge graph your organisation keeps.

Forge perturbs that structure, verifies it mathematically, and turns whatever it finds missing into the next capability.

Which returns to Intent Studio, better armed. Every intent after the first one starts richer than the last: it does not re-establish the lead time, re-litigate the part, or re-run the validation. That is the compounding asset.

Why it compounds

The substrate appreciates while the models depreciate.

01

Navigate the subspace instead of paying for the search

Once a successful logic path is indexed into a compiled playbook, the enterprise stops brute-forcing tokens to rediscover it. It navigates straight to a pre-verified causal pathway. Inference cost and latency fall as a function of how much work you have already done — not how much compute you can buy.

02

Commodity models, compounding substrate

GPT, Claude and open weights are swappable and will keep commoditising. Model choice is a role in the product, configurable per deployment, not a support ticket. Value is captured below the model layer, where your knowledge graph and operational memory belong to you and grow indefinitely.

03

Verification a regulator can actually accept

Formal mathematical verification produces the auditability that low-tolerance industries require — quantitative finance, biotech, aerospace — where a probabilistic hallucination is not a bug report but a commercially unviable outcome.

The usual questions

What people ask in the first meeting.

Is this a wrapper around somebody else's model?

No. The models are pluggable and you choose which ones run, per deployment. What we build is the layer beneath them — the intent record, the policy gates, the audit trail and the knowledge graph. Swap the model and all of that survives.

Does anything leave our network?

In the self-hosted deployment, no. It runs in your cloud against your models, your identity provider and your audit store, licensed by key with no telemetry and no phone-home. Air-gapped operation is supported.

Do we have to adopt all four products?

No. Each is complete and worth buying on its own. Most organisations start with one — either bounding a single decision type in Intent Studio, or getting visibility of existing AI traffic through Precepta — and compose the rest later.

Why not just wait for the models to get good enough?

Because the gap is not capability. Alignment inside the weights is uncomputable for expressive systems, so a better model still cannot tell you why it was allowed to act. That has to be enforced outside the model, and it has to be recorded.
