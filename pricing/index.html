There is no hosted Precepta, and there will not be one. It governs the boundary, so it has to sit inside it. These three are priced in conversation because two organisations running the same product can need very different environments, retention and assurance — we would rather tell you what it costs for your scope than publish a number that is wrong for almost everybody.

Deployment is quoted separately

Deployment and onboarding are quoted as their own line, never folded into the licence, so you can see what you are paying for and what it is for. Policies, connected models and people who only read the record are never metered.
