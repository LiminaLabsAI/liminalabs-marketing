No meter on these

What we never charge for.

Some things get more valuable the more you use them. Pricing that discouraged coverage would make the product worse, so we do not tax those.

Policies

Write as many as your governance needs. A rule you left out to save money is a rule that was not enforced.

Models

Connect as many as you want. Being able to leave a model vendor cheaply is the whole point, so we do not charge you for the option.

People who only read

Only contributors count. An incomplete record is worth less to you than a complete one.

Deployment is quoted separately

Deployment and onboarding are quoted as their own line, never folded into the licence, so you can see what you are paying for and what it is for.

Why we price in conversation

Two organisations running the same product can need very different environments, retention and assurance. We would rather tell you what it costs for your scope than publish a number that is wrong for almost everybody.
