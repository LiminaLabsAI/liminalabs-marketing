Company

We build the part of AI you should not rent.

Companies are putting AI into real work and finding they cannot say what it was supposed to do, cannot stop it doing something else, and cannot prove what it did. The model itself is rented and replaceable. What your company is trying to do, what it is allowed to do and what it has learned should not be — so we build those outside the model, where changing a prompt does not change them.

Limina is the Latin plural of limen: thresholds. A threshold is not a wall — things cross it, in both directions — and it still marks exactly where one place stops and another begins.

What we believe

Owning your AI is not about hosting the model. It is about owning the thinking around it. The model is rented and easy to swap. What should not be swappable is what your company is trying to do, what it is allowed to do, and what it has learned. Put the limits outside the model, where it cannot talk its way around them, and the rest follows.
