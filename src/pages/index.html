The problem

Agents don't fail because models are weak. They fail because they assume.

Give an agent a vague task and it fills the gaps with guesses. Nobody sees the guesses. The work comes out confidently wrong, and it is usually found late.

Everyone works from a different picture

People paste context into a chat assistant one at a time. Each person explains it their own way, nobody writes it down, and the gaps get filled with different assumptions.

One person starts, the team finds out later

Someone prompts a coding agent and it changes real things. The people who should have had a say see it afterwards, when undoing it costs more than agreeing would have.

The rules are written down, but nothing enforces them

AI rules live in a policy document. The AI never reads it. Nobody can see what the agents actually did, and company data can leave the building without anyone noticing.

This is not a small problem yet to be noticed. Gartner expects more than 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by the end of 2027. IBM found that 97% of breached organisations with an AI-related incident had no proper AI access controls.

Where it fits

Other tools solve part of this. None of them solve it together.

Chat and coding agents

They act on one person's prompt, in someone else's cloud. Fast for that person. The rest of the team finds out afterwards.

Team tools with AI added

Shared documents and tickets, with AI on top. Good for writing things down. They do not settle what the work actually is before an agent starts on it.

AI gateways and governance tools

They filter or record AI traffic. Useful, and necessary. But they sit outside the work, so they never see what the team meant to do.

What is different here. Agents act on what the whole team agreed, not on one person's prompt. The place where the work is agreed and the thing that governs the AI are the same system — so what was decided and what happened sit in one record. And both can be deployed inside your own environment, which none of the tools above offer.
