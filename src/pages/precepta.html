What it does

One path, and everything takes it.

There is no bypass route. A request either goes through the whole path or it does not run.

Firewall → Sensitivity → Policy → Smart Route → Cache → Inference → Audit

Boundary

Zero-egress by construction

Signed sovereignty attestation and continuous live egress probes. A proof, not a promise.

Record

Tamper-evident audit

Every request, decision and output recorded with lineage. The answer when compliance asks what happened.

Policy

Hard limits

Per-key scopes and cost and token caps, enforced outside the model where it cannot argue past them.

Models

Smart routing

Across open-weight and hosted models. Swap the muscle, keep the mind.

Redaction

Data protection

PII redaction before the model sees it; output scanned for leaks on the way back.

Interface

Drop-in

OpenAI-compatible API — chat, embeddings, moderations. Point existing code at it and change almost nothing.

How it runs

We hold none of it.

Not "we handle your data carefully" — there is no environment of ours for it to sit in. Models, gateway, policy and the audit record are all inside your network.

Self-hosted

Runs inside your cloud account or data centre, with no public route. Nothing reaches us — there is no telemetry and no phone-home.

On-premises

Including networks with no egress at all. The same artifact runs in both, so what you evaluate is what you operate.

Air-gapped

Networks with no egress at all are the case this was designed for, not an exception it tolerates.

Zero-egress is proven where it matters, not on a web page. It needs the software inside your network, so it is demonstrated during an evaluation on your own infrastructure — with your security team choosing the tests.

Who it's for

CIOs, CISOs and platform teams.

The people who have to answer for what the AI touched — and who already own the infrastructure it should be running on.

How we price it

By environment, then by throughput.

Zero-egress means usage can never be metered remotely. Capacity is agreed at contract and reconciled from your own audit logs — a property of the sovereignty model, not a limitation of the billing system.
