Pricing

We charge for contributors. Everyone else is free.

Viewers, reviewers, attestors, approvers, evaluators and guests cost nothing, on every tier. The people whose participation makes the governance record worth having should never be a per-seat negotiation.

We have not published numbers yet, and that is deliberate. We're in early access and pricing with our first customers rather than guessing at it. The structure below is settled; the figures are not. Tell us the process you want to run and we'll price it in the first conversation.

What each product is charged on

Seats alone would be the wrong unit for this company. Intent Studio's direction is to carry out work with its own agents, so a pure seat model would mean revenue falling as the product succeeds. Each product has a way in, and a way it scales with what it is worth.

ProductWay inScales on
Intent StudioContributor seatsGoverned processes in production
PreceptaEnvironments and deploymentsThroughput band, declared at contract
Cerebrio—Runs, retention window, versions
Precepta cannot be metered remotely, by design. Zero-egress means we never see your usage. Capacity is declared at contract and reconciled from your own audit logs at renewal. That is a property of the sovereignty model, not a limitation of the billing system.

Step one is a fixed-fee pilot

One process, on your infrastructure, with success criteria agreed in writing and in numbers before we start. If it works, an annual licence. If it doesn't, you stop and owe nothing further.
