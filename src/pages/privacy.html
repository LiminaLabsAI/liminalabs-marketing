Privacy

The short version is that there is very little to tell you, which is the intended outcome rather than an oversight.

This website

No third-party analytics, no advertising or marketing pixels, no hosted chat widget, no social embeds, and no cookies set by us. Typefaces are served from our own origin rather than a font CDN, so loading a page here does not tell anyone else that you did. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Self-hosted deployments

Nothing reaches us. There is no telemetry and no phone-home. Prompts, completions, embeddings and audit records stay in your infrastructure, and we have no means of reading them.

We operate no environment that holds your data

There is no hosted tier and no managed service. Precepta and Intent Studio run on your infrastructure, which means there is no system of ours for your data to sit in — not a commitment we are asking you to trust, but an absence you can verify.

If you contact us

If you apply for early access or email us, we keep what you sent so we can reply. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for.
