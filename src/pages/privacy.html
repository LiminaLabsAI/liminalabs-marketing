Privacy

This website

No analytics, no ad or marketing pixels, no chat widget, no social embeds, and no cookies from us. Even the fonts come from our own server, so opening this page tells nobody else that you did. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to agree to.

Deployed in your own environment

This covers Precepta always, and Intent Studio when you deploy it yourself. Nothing reaches us. The software never calls home. Everything it handles and everything it records stays on your infrastructure, and we have no way to read any of it. That is not a promise you have to trust — it is something you can check.

Intent Studio, hosted by us

Intent Studio is also available hosted, so a team can sign up and start without deploying anything. If you use it that way, your workspaces, intents and the record of what was agreed are held on our infrastructure rather than yours. We do not sell that data, and we do not use it to train models. If that is not acceptable for your work, deploy Intent Studio in your own environment instead — it is the same product.

Precepta has no hosted version, and will not have one. It only ever runs on your own infrastructure.

If you email us, we keep what you sent so we can reply. We do not sell it, and we will not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.
