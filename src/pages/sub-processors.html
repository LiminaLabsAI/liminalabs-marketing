Sub-processors

Short, because self-hosted. Precepta and Intent Studio run on your infrastructure, so there are no sub-processors at all — there is no data flow for one to sit in.

Self-hosted and on-premises

None. Your data never reaches our infrastructure, so no third party processes it on our behalf. This is a property of the deployment model rather than a commitment we are asking you to trust.

If that ever changes

If we introduce anything that processes customer data on our side, it will be listed here in full, and published before it takes effect rather than after.
