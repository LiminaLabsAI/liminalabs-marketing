Terms

Three different things are covered by different agreements, and it is worth being clear about which is which.

This website

Provided as-is, for information. Nothing on it is an offer, a warranty, or a commitment to deliver any particular capability. Where a page describes what a product does, the written agreement covering your deployment is what governs.

Evaluations

An evaluation runs on your own infrastructure under a written agreement covering scope, duration and what happens at the end of it. Nothing about an evaluation creates an ongoing licence, and nothing on this website varies what that agreement says.

Licensed software

Precepta and Intent Studio are licensed under a written agreement signed by both parties. That agreement governs — not this page. It covers the licence grant, support, service levels, liability and source escrow where escrow is agreed.
