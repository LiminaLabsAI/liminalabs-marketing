Security and sovereignty

The whole stack runs on your infrastructure.

Not the interface with the reasoning elsewhere — all of it. Models, gateway, policy, audit and the record itself, inside your own network, over whichever models you choose to run. Nothing on this page is gated.

01

Zero-egress architecture

The boundary is a property of where the software sits, not a setting inside it. Under self-hosting, Precepta runs in your cloud account or data centre with no public route. Model inference happens in-boundary. There is no telemetry and no phone-home, so there is no channel over which your data could reach us even if we wanted it.

That is the whole argument, and it is structural: we cannot lose data we never receive.

02

What we hold, and what we don't

Under a self-hosted deployment: nothing. No prompts, no completions, no embeddings, no audit records, no customer data of any kind. Your audit store is yours and stays in your infrastructure.

There is no exception, because there is no hosted tier. We do not operate an environment your data could sit in, which is a stronger statement than a policy promising we would handle it carefully if we did.

03

Attestation and live probes

Precepta issues a signed sovereignty attestation, and continuous live probes test the egress boundary rather than asserting it. This is the part of the system we most want you to distrust on principle — which is why it is demonstrated during your evaluation, on your own network, with your team choosing the tests, rather than shown as a sample here.

Our intent is to open-source the verifier so an attestation can be checked with code we did not write. That does not exist yet, and we will not describe it as though it does.

04

Tamper-evident audit

Every request, policy decision, redaction and output is recorded with lineage, so a specific action can be reconstructed after the fact — what was asked, what policy said, what the model saw, and what came back. Integrity is chained, so a record cannot be altered after the event without the alteration being evident.

05

Dependencies are scanned, releases are signed, and the same artifact runs in an evaluation, in a self-hosted deployment and on-premises — so what you evaluate is precisely what you operate. Our patching commitment and disclosure timelines are in the security overview below.

08

Any model, and you are never locked to ours

Precepta is a gateway, not a model vendor. Run open-weight models in your own boundary — Llama and its peers, on your hardware — or reach a hosted provider where your platform owner has explicitly approved that exact host. Both paths go through the same governed pipeline, and the choice is yours to change at any time.

This is the point of putting the boundary outside the model. A better model arrives every few months; you should be able to take it without re-architecting anything. What stays is the policy, the audit record and the accumulated intent — which is the part you actually own.

09

Documents — none of them gated

No form, no email capture, no sales call in the way. Security documents behind a lead form are a tell, and your team should not have to negotiate for them.

Sixty to ninety minutes on a live deployment, where they pick what to try — including the exfiltration attempt. We do not get to set the questions.
